Common Questions

Everything you want to know

Is there a cost for an initial consultation?+

No — the initial 30-minute Financial Clarity Session is completely free. There is no obligation to proceed, and no pressure. It's simply a conversation to understand your current situation and whether Kris can add genuine value.

I'm just starting out financially. Is it too early to see an advisor?+

Absolutely not — in fact, starting early is one of the biggest advantages you can have. Kris works with clients at all stages, from those just starting their first job to those approaching retirement. Early planning means more time for compounding to work in your favour.

How is Kris different from other financial advisors?+

Kris operates under Advisors Alliance Group — the only advisory group in Southeast Asia with 8 consecutive 100-IDA honours. Beyond credentials, Kris is known for clear, jargon-free advice with no pressure selling. The 97% five-star review rate reflects a consistent commitment to client outcomes over product commissions.

What areas does Kris advise on?+

Kris covers the full spectrum of personal financial planning: insurance and protection, investments and portfolio management, retirement planning (CPF optimisation, SRS, drawdown strategies), estate planning (wills, nominations, trusts), tax planning, and cashflow management.

How long does it take to get a financial plan in place?+

Most clients have a clear, actionable financial plan within 1–2 weeks of the first consultation. The process involves a detailed needs analysis, reviewing existing policies and holdings, and presenting a personalised strategy tailored to your goals and risk profile.

How do I get started?+

The easiest way is to WhatsApp Kris directly at +65 9271 6951. Just say "I'd like a free 30-minute session" and Kris will arrange a time that works for you — either in person or via video call.
